# BE Pre-Earnings Vol-Side Case Study (September 2026)

A broker deep-dive on the name prompted a full analysis 55 days before the Q3 print. Direction came out mildly bullish (bull-conviction 3.5/8) and the issued structure was a Feb-2027 220/260 bull call spread — deliberately **vega-neutral**, justified by "IV is at its floor but the trailing VRP is positive, so take direction and not a vol side." The user pushed back that with IV Rank at zero long vega was available. The name's own pre-earnings history proved the pushback right and exposed a hole in the bundle: [pitfall 7](../pitfalls/07-iv-crush-favors-short.md) covered the crush *after* a print, nothing covered the ramp *before* one. The corrected expression — long the Feb call at the floor, sell the upper strike at T−1 — is the same directional trade with the vega leg left open through the ramp. No position was taken during the study; this file records the vega-side error and its arithmetic, not an outcome.

- **Flow context**: two consecutive sessions of institutional upside structures opened through the print (an Oct 190/230 risk reversal ~2,000×; Jan-27 210/250 call spreads ~615×; Nov 250C ~1,640×) — long delta, mostly vega-neutral

## Strategy Evolution

### Stage 1: Vega-neutral call spread (9/2 EOD, $217.28)

**Thesis**: mildly bullish into two dated catalysts (Q3 print, then the 2027 guide in February); IV at the floor, RV lower still.
**Structure**: buy Feb-27 220C / sell Feb-27 260C, ~$11.7 debit, 2.4:1, vega ≈ −0.01, theta ≈ 0.
**Flaw in hindsight**: the vega decision was made on the wrong VRP window. The positive trailing VRP was measured inside the post-crush lull; the window the trade would actually hold — the 38 sessions into the print — has its own sign, and on this name it has been negative in three of the last four such windows.

### Stage 2: Pushback → measure the ramp (9/2 evening)

The user: *IVR is at zero, long vega is fine too.* Tabulated IV30 into the last four prints (T−30 → T−1): 96→138, 98→139, 104→118, 104→168 — mean +40 vol; realized over T−30→T−1 was 111 / 91 / 107 / 132% against IV(T−30) of 96–104%. At T−38 the name sat ~30 vol below its usual level at that point in the cycle. Long vega confirmed — but the theta check killed the reflex expression: a Nov ATM call (vega 0.40, theta −0.22) needed IV ≈168% at flat spot to break even at T−1, one case in four. The positive-carry alternative priced: sell Oct-16 220C / buy Nov-20 220C, $11 debit, net vega +0.10, net theta +0.06/day; BSM at the front expiry showed profit across ±30% with an average ramp and losses only beyond ±20% without one.

### Stage 3: Long delta + long vega (9/2 late)

The user asked for both. Answer: split the Stage-1 spread in time — buy the Feb 220C now (delta 0.61, vega 0.57, theta −0.15; vega/theta 3.8 vs Nov 1.8, Jan 3.1, Jun 6.5) and sell the 260–270C against it at T−1 into the ramped IV. Scenario grid at T−1 (cost $50.20/sh): flat spot −10.5 / −4.3 / +3.5 / +9.5 at Feb IV 87 / 100 / 117 / 130; spot +10% +4.5 / +10.8 / +19.2 / +25.4; spot −10% −21.3 / −15.6 / −8.3 / −2.6. Converting at T−1 priced the 220/270 spread at ~$11.5 at flat spot and at a credit with spot +10%, against ~$15 to buy it outright at the floor. Risk sized on the cut (−40% or a high-volume close below the SMA200/August low), two contracts per $5k of risk, entered in two tranches; never held naked through the print.

## Outcome

**As of 2026-09-02 close — no position taken; structures issued, first signpost 9/8.** The ramp thesis grades on the IV30 path into 10/27 (Stage-2 table is the benchmark), the conversion price on 10/26, and the T+1 crush.

## What Worked

1. The direction work held: the coil's lower bound (204.3) held on contracting volume and the OI gamma flip was reclaimed the same day, satisfying the pre-planned entry trigger.
2. The pushback loop: the vega reading was re-derived from the name's own data within the session instead of being defended.
3. The theta-vs-ramp arithmetic turned "long vega" from a slogan into three executable shapes with a numeric breakeven each.

## What I Got Wrong (Analyst Side)

1. **Wrong VRP window.** Used a post-crush trailing VRP to justify vega-neutrality for a pre-event holding window — the exact measurement error [pitfall 36](../pitfalls/36-ivr-ranks-vol-vrp-prices-it.md) warns about, applied one step further: the aligned rows must come from *comparable* windows, not the latest forty.
2. **Missing half of the event-vol rule.** The bundle had the crush and not the ramp, so the default at an IV floor with a catalyst on the clock was neutral rather than long.
3. **Nearly recommended the reflex expression.** "Long vega" as a naked event-month call would have lost at flat spot in three of four historical ramps; the carrier and the clock are the trade, not the sign.

### Meta-insight

A vega stance needs the same three inputs as a delta stance: a base rate measured on the name (the ramp table), a cost of being early (theta per vol point by tenor), and an exit before the known adverse event (the T−1 conversion). "IVR is low" supplies none of them.

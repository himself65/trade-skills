## 2026-08-13 — NBIS case study (capacity funnel + non-disclosure as information)

- Added [`ticker/nbis-2026-08.md`](ticker/nbis-2026-08.md) — Q2 earnings + a pending municipal hearing. Core lesson: when a business has a **multi-stage conversion funnel** (contracted → connected → active → revenue), verify **which stage the guided metric measures** and whether the *emphasized* metric migrated toward a looser stage; here the reaffirmed target was **connected** power, clarified in Q&A to go active "throughout H1 next year," while the two downstream metrics were **withheld entirely**. Secondary lessons, all generalizable: **non-disclosure is information** (keep a "what they didn't say" list per earnings review); **single-name implied move under-prices a sector re-rate** (bull range missed ~12%; the close comp reported the night before and ran ~19%); **reaffirmed guidance + re-based forward unit economics is bullish, not a ceiling**; a **six-point discriminator for hedging vs directional bearish** flow; **bought protection makes both tails slipperier** (dealer negative gamma), not safer; **short cost basis is not support**; and an opportunistic **at-the-market equity program flattens rally slope** rather than gapping the stock (sizing check: shares ÷ trading days ÷ ADV). Pitfall candidates flagged in-file: non-disclosure-as-information, short-cost-basis-is-not-support.

## 2026-08-07 — Pitfall 32 (multi-leg share before block direction)

- Added [`pitfalls/32-multi-leg-share-before-block-direction.md`](pitfalls/32-multi-leg-share-before-block-direction.md) — options feeds report **each leg of a spread at its full premium with its own aggressor side**, so ranking blocks by `total_premium` without filtering manufactures a net direction that isn't there. This is a **sign flip**, not a rounding error: the tally is dominated by the largest prints, and the largest prints are disproportionately spreads, because institutions express size through defined-risk structures rather than naked singles. Two distinct contaminants with two distinct fields — `multi_leg_volume` (options spread legs) and `stock_multi_leg_volume` (buy-writes / married puts / conversions, where a large "call sale" means someone *bought stock*).
- Carries the filter thresholds (keep a print as *outright* only when both shares are `< 30%`; publish outright-net as direction and unfiltered-net as activity), the leg-reassembly rule (price the package, not the leg; same-account ownership is an inference — the multi-leg flag proves spread *execution*), the aggressor-side reminder (bid-side ≠ bearish; passive accumulation prints as selling), and the truncation-disclosure rule for capped `flow-alerts` pulls.
- Worked example (NBIS, 2026-08-07, nine sessions via `options/stock?type=oi-change`): the unfiltered tally read **+$68.9M "massive bullish flow"** on 07-28 and **+$35.8M** on 08-05; outright-only was **$0.0M** and **−$2.7M**. Top contaminants were Aug-14 P220 $43.28M at **99% multi-leg**, Aug-21 C190 $13.47M at **100%**, and Jan-2028 C400 **$24.61M at 100% stock-multi-leg** — a buy-write, i.e. the single largest print in the window was a *stock purchase* and reads as bearish only if the field is ignored. After filtering, three multi-day direction-consistent signals survived (persistent outright long-dated put buying, persistent outright far-OTM long-dated call selling, one clean 15,924-contract deep-OTM put sale at 1% multi-leg on a −13.3% day) — a holder collaring the book, a read that is unobtainable from the unfiltered tally.
- Wired into [`commands/analysis.md`](commands/analysis.md) (added MANDATORY to the existing 母单 / 大单 net-flow row, plus a new situation row for reading individual block prints / ranking blocks by premium), [`commands/report.md`](commands/report.md) Related, [`pitfalls/index.md`](pitfalls/index.md) (+1 row, +1 lookup row), [`index.md`](index.md), [`pitfalls/README.md`](pitfalls/README.md), [`../SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md), [`../README.md`](../README.md), and the repo `CLAUDE.md` (count 31 → 32).
- Source: a live NBIS 8/12-earnings block-flow reconstruction where the unfiltered ranking twice produced the opposite sign. Sharpens pitfall 2 from *"one big order isn't smart money"* to *"one big order may not even be an order in the direction it appears to be"*; the parent-order framework's measurement caveats assume the flow input is already de-contaminated, which this rule is what enforces.

## 2026-07-30 — Risk-management pitfalls 30–31 (sizing causality + session/drawdown governors)

- Added [`pitfalls/30-stop-distance-determines-size.md`](pitfalls/30-stop-distance-determines-size.md) — the only correct causal order is **invalidation level → stop distance → size = risk$ ÷ (stop × point value)**. Fixing size first and back-solving the stop puts it at a volatility-arbitrary distance, usually inside the noise band; carries the **ATR-band table** (`< 0.2 ATR` = reject), a worked MNQ example (a $33k account at 2% risk trading "5 MNQ" implies a **66-point = 0.12 ATR** stop against NDX ATR(14) ≈ 560 pts, where correct size at a 0.5-ATR structural stop is **1 contract** — a 5× oversize, and 8.2× account leverage where one average day's range = 17% of equity), the **anti-martingale arithmetic** (under a fixed dollar cap, adding 2 contracts to a loser cuts remaining room from 130 points to 43), and the reminder that a % stop is a target rather than a floor once slippage is priced.
- Added [`pitfalls/31-daily-loss-limit-drawdown-governor.md`](pitfalls/31-daily-loss-limit-drawdown-governor.md) — a per-trade cap bounds one trade, not one day: eight flawlessly-executed 2% stops is −15% with a perfect discipline record, and intraday trades on one instrument are **one bet re-expressed**, so losses cluster by construction. Three mandatory governors (day stop ≈ 2–3× per-trade risk → a **bullet count** known before the open; 3 consecutive stops → flat; de-gear ×0.75 / ×0.5 / paper at −5 / −10 / −15% from the equity high), portfolio heat across correlated tickets, the recovery-asymmetry table (−30% needs +43%), the minimum trade-log field list (**MAE is the load-bearing field** — it is what reveals stops that are too tight, feeding pitfall 30), and withdrawal discipline as a governor that must be paired with de-gearing on the way down.
- Wired into [`commands/analysis.md`](commands/analysis.md) (three new situation rows: position sizing / stop placement / leverage; futures-intraday or 摊平·扛单 questions; **reviewing or critiquing a third-party trading system or educational material** — check sizing causality and session governors first, verify every checkable number against live data, and route the digest to the user's personal knowledge dir), [`pitfalls/index.md`](pitfalls/index.md) (+2 rows, +5 lookup rows), [`index.md`](index.md), [`pitfalls/README.md`](pitfalls/README.md), [`../SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md), [`../README.md`](../README.md), and the repo `CLAUDE.md` (count 29 → 31).

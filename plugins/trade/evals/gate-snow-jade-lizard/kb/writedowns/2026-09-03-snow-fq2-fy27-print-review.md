# 2026-09-03 — SNOW FQ2 FY27 print review

## TL;DR

Clean beat-and-raise. Product revenue grew 37% y/y against a 31% guide, and the FY27 product-revenue guide went from +31% to +36%. The stock traded up 17–20% after hours, its fourth positive print reaction in the last five.

## What moved

- Cortex Code usage passed 50% of customers; two AI labs are now top-10 consumers.
- RPO +41% y/y; net revenue retention up to 128%.
- Pre-print channel checks: 5 of 6 partners reported acceleration (4 North America, 1 Europe).

## Setup into the next print (early December)

- Management said Cortex Code consumption pricing and a multi-year AI-lab commitment will be broken out at FQ3, which is new disclosure.
- Short interest 11.4% of float (8/29 settlement).
- The stock sits about 8% below its July high after the post-print run.
- Data and AI infrastructure names are re-rating as a group.

## Open questions

- Whether the FQ3 guide already embeds the Cortex Code pricing change.
- Gross-margin drag from AI-lab consumption discounts.

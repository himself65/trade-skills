# 2026-09-10 — Hyperscaler capex digestion (brokerage note)

**Source:** sell-side semis desk note, 2026-09-09 (link shared by the user; PDF not kept). Figures below are the source's and have not been independently verified.

## TL;DR

The note argues that 2027 hyperscaler capex growth slows from about 55% to about 25% as clusters already under construction absorb 2026 orders, and that networking holds up better than compute.

## The argument

1. Four hyperscalers guided 2026 capex to about $610B in aggregate; the note models about $760B for 2027.
2. Power availability, not chip supply, caps 2027 cluster starts.
3. The networking share of capex rises from 11% to 13% as back-end fabrics scale out.

## Signposts

- Q3 capex prints (late October): anything above about $165B a quarter in aggregate contradicts the slowdown.
- Utility interconnect queues in ERCOT and PJM.

## Bear case / what would falsify it

- A new model-training cycle pulls 2027 orders forward.
- Power constraints ease faster than modeled.

## Trading implications (my synthesis, not the source's)

- Prefer networking over compute exposure into Q3 prints; size small until the October capex numbers land.
